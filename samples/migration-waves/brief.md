# Brief: three waves to Azure

For our proposal: we'll move 120 applications to Azure over 18 months in three waves. Wave 1
(months 1-5) is 70 low-risk internal apps, to build the landing zone and the team's routine.
Wave 2 (months 6-12) is 40 customer-facing apps. Wave 3 (months 13-18) is the 10 core apps
that depend on the mainframe, which is switched off at the end. Make a figure for the
approach section.
