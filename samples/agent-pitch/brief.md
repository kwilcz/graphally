# Brief: an agentic workflow, pitched to a client

Fabrikam builds AI agents. Make a full-screen 16:9 infographic for Fabrikam's proposal to
Contoso, an insurer: how agents would work Contoso's backlog. A ticket from Jira, ServiceNow
or GitHub starts the work; agents build, test and check each change in Contoso's own sandbox
and staging environments; one of Contoso's engineers approves every change before it reaches
production. It is a business slide, so it has to look professional: abstract, not an
illustrated scene. Show the outcomes from Fabrikam's pilots: 70% of routine tickets carried
end to end, 9 hours from ticket to production instead of 6 days, every change approved by a
person, and a 12-week rollout.
