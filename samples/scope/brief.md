# Brief: what this proposal covers

For the scope section of the same proposal to Northwind Insurance: what the claims
platform work includes, what is offered as an option for a second phase, and what is not
part of the work, across four areas: Engage, Process, Data and Run. A reader should see
at a glance where the line is drawn.

- In scope: the mobile app and the customer portal (Engage); claims intake and automated
  triage (Process); the document store and the migration of the claims data (Data);
  monitoring around the clock and the security baseline (Run).
- Phase 2 options, each priced and planned separately: a chat assistant, fraud scoring,
  management dashboards, cost control.
- Out of scope: the broker portal, underwriting, a data warehouse, office laptops.
- The headline numbers: eight capabilities in scope at a fixed price, live in nine months;
  four options for phase 2.
