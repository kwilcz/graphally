# Brief: how we work together

A one-page figure for the governance section of a proposal to Northwind Insurance: how
the client and Contoso Digital, the delivery partner, run the claims programme together.
It goes in a sales document, so it should look designed, not like boxes and arrows.

- Four levels on each side, the most senior at the top:
  - Northwind: the executive sponsor (the CIO); the programme director (Head of Claims);
    product owners for Claims, Customer and Finance; the business and IT teams (claims
    handlers, IT operations, testers).
  - Contoso Digital: the account executive (a partner of the insurance practice); the
    engagement manager (plan, budget, risks); the delivery lead and architects (solution,
    quality, backlog); the engineering squads (two squads of six and a platform engineer).
- Each level meets its counterpart: a steering committee every quarter, a programme board
  every month, a sprint review every two weeks, a stand-up every day.
- Issues escalate one level at a time, within two working days per level; decisions and
  priorities come down from the top.
