# Brief: insurance claim process (BPMN)

Model our insurance claim process in BPMN for the process handbook. A customer submits a
claim online. Claims intake checks it for completeness; if documents are missing, intake
requests them from the customer and waits up to 14 days, after which the claim is closed.
Complete claims are scored automatically for fraud: high-risk claims go to the special
investigations unit, the rest to an adjuster. The adjuster assesses the damage; claims over
10,000 EUR need a team lead's approval. Approved claims are paid by finance and the customer
is notified; rejected claims are notified to the customer with the reason.
